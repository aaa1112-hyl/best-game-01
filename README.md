## The game

**Lumen Keep** — the shadows march on the last lighthouse on the coast.
You are its keeper. Build lantern towers on the plots beside the shadow
road, spend the gold the fallen leave behind, and hold the light through
all 12 waves — the last one brings the Gloomlord himself.

- **Four towers:** Ember Turret (fast and cheap), Frost Lantern (slows the
  horde), Cannon (splash damage), Arc Coil (lightning that jumps to three
  foes). Each upgrades twice and can be sold back for 70%.
- **Five shadows:** wisps, crawlers, dusk bats, brutes and the Gloomlord,
  with their own speed, health and bounty; every wave mixes them harder.
- **A real loop:** build phase, wave, clear bonus, next wave — win by
  surviving wave 12, lose when the lighthouse falls. Score from kills,
  gold and the lives you kept.
- **Feel:** a rotating lighthouse beam, glowing shots, lightning arcs,
  particle bursts, floating gold, and synthesised WebAudio sound (shoot,
  boom, coin, horn, win and lose) with M to mute. Space starts a wave or
  toggles 2× speed, P pauses, 1–4 pick a tower.
